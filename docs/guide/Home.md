# e-Handkerchief guide

e-Handkerchief is a mobile-first, offline-first PWA for tying **knots**: quick, location-aware reminders you capture by voice, photo, video or text. Everything is stored on your device; Google Drive backup and voice transcription are optional extras.

## For users

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): install the app, the home-icon shortcut and the quick-capture notification.
- [Capturing knots](capturing.md): location, photos and videos, audio files, Mic, Dictate and transcription.
- [Organising knots](organising.md): the Knots list, search, the Calendar, checking off, Random.
- [Sharing](sharing.md): share one knot or several.
- [Cloud backup](cloud-backup.md): Google Drive sync, Merge with Cloud, conflict review, Manage backups.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): every message the app can show, and what to do about it.

## For people running their own copy

- [Self-hosting](self-hosting.md): build, deploy to GitHub Pages, the two optional Cloudflare Workers, Google OAuth, forks and custom domains.

## For contributors

See `CONTRIBUTING.md` in the repository. The spec of record is `.kiro/specs/e-handkerchief/` (`requirements.md` and `design.md`).
